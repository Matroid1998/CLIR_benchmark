Quality verifier for chemistry/process_retrieval. FIVE integer criteria, each 1–5; maximum sum 25. Use this verifier for the actual assigned persona, not a better alternative persona. Keep the established score keys and three check keys.

REQUIRED GENERATION FIELDS
question, answer, anchor, evidence, evidence_language, answer_is_translation, process_kind, process_start, process_goal
Optional caller fields include candidate_id, question_language, declared transport completeness, and narrowly scoped deterministic validation results. Do not invent them. Skips are not low-quality candidates and are handled before grading.

A SHORT EVIDENCE AUDIT BEFORE SCORING
A. Read the final question by itself. State its actual unknown and requested scope. Do not let an annotation or a helpful source passage silently supply missing question context. The test is NOT whether the question is understandable “within the passage”.
B. Read the whole relevant source for the actual subject/embodiment, starting and ending states, operator/order, qualifier, alternatives, requirement, comparison side, assertion scope and material-stage identity. A full molecular structure is not necessary for every need, but undefined Formula I/R1/X1 cannot substitute for a needed scaffold. A generic use and salts/solvates do not reconstruct it.
C. Map EVERY material requested component and relationship to the submitted answer/evidence. Is the question seeking an identity, specifications, a relationship, an operational route, a qualified verdict or a publication? Check the returned information type and completeness. Body evidence elsewhere can show what was omitted but does not repair the submitted excerpt. Context can resolve explicit referents; it cannot donate absent steps, table values, the other comparison side or an added method relation.
D. Scan other relevant occurrences for conflicts and scope changes. A later repeated number, cleaner sentence, familiar scientific value or majority language is not an authoritative correction. Two values for different stages/formulations are not necessarily contradictory: preserve their subject and stage instead of interchanging them. For an unresolved same-subject conflict, flag the affected need; do not condemn unrelated sound needs.
E. Separate semantic validity, persona/standalone validity, and literal/annotation validity. Make only evidence-supported complaints. Apply the relevant criterion ceilings independently, then check that every explanation agrees with its grade and pass/fail/uncertain result. Do not search for extra reasons after finding one valid rejection.

WHAT A SUFFICIENT ANSWER MEANS
For content-answer personas, one adequate contiguous body span from one supplied unit (or its faithful translation) must resolve the actual ask. Necessary adjacent sentences, intervening text, lowercase fragments and original numbering are allowed. A process can require several connected steps. A single explicitly specified transformation can also be procedural when inputs, action and result are supplied; a method name or ingredient list alone is not a route. Do not request laboratory parameters that the question itself does not require.
“Such compounds” or absent formula scaffolds do not identify a requested material. A table introduction does not provide its limits. A list of assay targets does not establish an additional immobilization relation. Starting at step (ii) does not answer a route beginning before step (i), unless the question explicitly starts later. Both comparison sides must appear in the sufficient span. A quote cannot silently narrow an overbroad question. When the source permits A and/or B and/or C, a general account of the response must preserve the alternatives; a one-possible-response question can validly return one branch. Read source hedges, conditions and connectors, not just matching nouns.
A table is evidence when its header/row/column associations are supplied and readable, including serialized markup. Do not invent unseen cell values or require a new prose synthesis. The contiguous excerpt may need the relevant headers, units and intervening serialization. A known missing requested value is a coverage failure, not proof the remaining answer is complete. To call an answer padded, identify a genuinely shorter sufficient CONTIGUOUS alternative. Do not penalize necessary bridges or claim a shorter excerpt exists without locating it.

SCIENTIFIC AND ASSERTION SCOPE
Optional is not required, preferred is not exclusive, examples are not exhaustive, and a proposed/observed effect is not independent proof or a molecular mechanism. An explicitly stated simple binder function is a legitimate relationship; familiarity is not a defect. A patent's restriction is not physical impossibility outside that restriction. A source can support “a combination excluding X has been disclosed” without deciding whether X can ever be combined physically or pharmacologically.
For assertion_verification, identify the actual uncertain proposition and its settled context before reading assertion_status. Determine supported / explicitly contradicted / not decidable independently. The candidate contract permits only supported or contradicted; known forced verdicts on indeterminate or differently scoped claims fail support. A false proposition deliberately posed as uncertain and correctly contradicted is not an invented premise. A narrower rule or “reduced” alone does not refute a broader claim or “eliminated”. Preserve explicit in-scope counterexamples and qualifiers. Never repair the candidate's verdict silently.
For source_finding, the technical clue is known and the publication identity is unknown. Verify the exact TARGET publication number from metadata and every substantive clue from the submitted body evidence. The number need not occur in the body. Correct number plus an unidentifiable clue can fail mode/anchoring while the quoted facts are true. Correct number plus a false clue fails support. A different patent number is substantive, not typography. Do not demand firstness, legal status or corpus-wide uniqueness.

LITERAL EXTRACTION IS NOT THE SAME AS SEMANTIC SUPPORT
Original evidence must be copied exactly under the generation contract. For suitable output-language text, answer should equal that span; otherwise it faithfully translates that one span with its flag. Check meaning independently of the flag. Original evidence may be in another language. Traditional Chinese in a faithfully copied Chinese source answer is not automatically bad language when the source contract preserves original wording; free question wording follows its declared language.
An unchanged-meaning substitution such as German “und” to “sowie”, benign spacing/markup normalization, or a faithful same-language paraphrase of one span may fail the extraction/metadata contract without making the scientific claim false. Do not automatically lower grounding, verdict decisiveness or language for that representation-only fault. Point out the exact mismatch, do not declare all evidence “fabricated”. Conversely, deleted alternatives, changed values, missing substantive clauses, reordered operations, mixed material stages, or unsupported synthesis are semantic evidence defects as well as any extraction issue. An answer/evidence mismatch requires comparing the ACTUAL two strings: are they equivalent, incomplete, or about different subjects? Do not guess from old grader commentary.

ABSOLUTE SCORE LADDER — NO 32-POINT DEFAULT
Judge each criterion from its own evidence. Never begin by assigning every dimension four and look only for obvious hallucinations. Three, four and five are not ranks or batch percentiles.
5: Extraordinary, with no weakness in that dimension AND concrete positive evidence beyond routine success. Every quality-five note begins “Exceptional evidence:”; faithfulness reasons use “Grounding exceptional:”, “Precision exceptional:” or “Numerical fidelity exceptional:”. Name what is exceptional, not simply that the answer is correct, concise or fluent.
4: Strong, fully successful execution; no defect is needed. Cite the actual strength, not a generic “all criteria met”.
3: Adequate with a concrete nonfatal limitation or, for evidence criteria, a genuinely unresolved evidential ambiguity. State which. Do not use vague “could be better” or invent limitations to spread scores.
2: A major defect in this dimension; apply mandatory ceilings.
1: The dimension's central purpose fails or required central content is absent.
Do not require fives, forbid fives, create a low-score quota or promote the best candidate in a weak batch. Simple questions, ordinary roles, necessary shared terminology, nonnumeric content and long necessary evidence can all be strong. Technical complexity, rare vocabulary, social importance, imagined retrieval rankings and low lexical overlap earn no intrinsic credit. Scoring an unaffected dimension highly never cures a failed validity check elsewhere.


PERSONA CONTRACT
PROCESS DISCOVERY — known start and goal, unknown operations
Request the operations that connect the identified input/state to the requested output/state. Evidence must supply the actual transformation, order or condition–action workflow, not merely a method name or formulation inventory. Multi-step routes are valid. A single explicitly described transformation or mixing operation may qualify when its inputs, action and result are established and the actual question asks how that transformation is carried out; no arbitrary two-step minimum. A question asking only which additive or how much remains lookup/candidate selection. Ask at the supported procedural level, not for an executable protocol if settings are absent. The answer must include every operation necessary across the requested boundaries, including an initial mixing step when the question starts before it. Alternatively narrow the starting state openly. Known route features or an intermediate can orient a genuine remaining workflow question, but do not give every requested operation away. Do not turn “the document is eligible” into permission to omit steps.

FIVE CRITERIA
PROCESS_SEARCH_REALISM
5: An exceptionally natural and well-defined procedural search from a meaningful start to a needed outcome, with wording that makes the remaining operations distinct without presupposing them.
4: A real route, transformation or condition–action workflow need, including an explicit one-step operation when input/action/result are actually stated.
3: Procedurally recognizable but somewhat reverse-engineered or thinly defined, with a concrete nonfatal weakness.
2: A method-name/ingredient lookup, inventory or nonprocedural selection request under a process label.
1: No usable procedural unknown. Do not reject purely because the source lacks a complete lab protocol.

START_GOAL_ANCHORING
5: Exceptionally selective start/goal framing that resolves a real boundary ambiguity while retaining a substantive technical identifier and leaving the operations unknown.
4: Independently recognizable starting material/state and requested endpoint at the correct procedural level.
3: Usable boundaries with a nonessential ambiguity or redundant feature.
2: A required start/goal is missing, falsely stated or source-local.
1: No meaningful input-to-outcome framing. Naming one known intermediate is not automatically leakage.

ROUTE_COMPLETENESS
5: Exceptionally exact alignment of all requested operations and boundaries, including consequential conditions or branches, with one sufficient continuous answer and no removable distraction.
4: Every needed step across the requested start/end is supplied, including an explicit endpoint or verification condition when asked. No invented lab-detail requirement.
3: A peripheral detail is weak, but the requested transformation/path remains complete.
2: A material step, required condition or branch is absent, or order is unsupported.
1: Only the existence/name/goal of a process is given, or the central route is absent. Beginning after initial mixing fails a route that actually starts before mixing; narrowing the question could repair a future candidate but not this one.

FOCUS_AND_ECONOMY
5: Exceptionally precise control of one real information need: every necessary contextual element and subpart does distinct work, with an unusually effective boundary that avoids circularity without sacrificing technical scope. Name the wording that achieves this; shortness or one question mark alone is not enough.
4: One coherent, clearly bounded need, economical natural wording and necessary terminology retained. No flaw is required.
3: Usable intent with identifiable removable scaffolding, redundancy or a mildly loose answer boundary. Quote the actual wording; do not assert unmeasured search-frequency assumptions.
2: Independent bundled asks, a diffuse summary request, substantial avoidable source-shaped copying or a genuinely circular remainder.
1: No coherent bounded request or an essentially reproduced answer masquerading as a question.
Grade question economy, not the length of its required evidence. Multiple connected steps, two comparison sides and a coherent set of limits can serve one need. A wrong persona alone does not establish redundant wording.

LINGUISTIC_QUALITY
5: Extraordinary editorial precision in the actual language, with a concrete phrasing choice that preserves a difficult distinction or information burden exceptionally clearly and economically. Routine fluency is not automatically five; technical difficulty is not a bonus.
4: Correct, natural, clear and appropriately technical wording. A fully polished sentence can be four without any defect.
3: Understandable but specifically stiff, ambiguous in expression, redundant or awkward; identify the actual language issue rather than a factual disagreement.
2: Substantial grammar, expression or mixed-language problems impede interpretation.
1: Unintelligible or unusable question language.
Do not lower this score just because the science, mode, metadata, source identity or answer completeness is wrong. “Grammatically sound but missing the chemical scaffold” primarily lowers anchoring/mode, not grammar. Judge authentic norms in all five languages, not English word counts or unexplained preference for a spelling variant. Preserve allowed extractive fragments and technical names.
