EVIDENCE CONTRACT
S = the supplied source units; Q = the actual question; A = the actual answer; E = submitted original evidence. A title or supplied context may identify a referent, but cannot invent a property or rescue missing standalone Q context. Read the relevant whole source, including adjacent provisos and competing occurrences; language variants are not independent corroboration. Do not merge separate units, alternative embodiments or language versions into one evidential span.
For six content-answer personas, E is one exact contiguous body span and A=E when answer_is_translation=false. When true, A is a faithful translation of that one span, not a summary or new synthesis. For source_finding, A is the exact supplied publication identifier and E separately substantiates the clue. No added yes/no, computed answer or explanation is required. Normalization is allowed only to the caller-declared extent. Harmless copying differences may violate representation without changing meaning.
Input availability matters. Missing required fields in a declared complete raw object are metadata failures. Explicitly omitted export fields are unassessable, not fabricated; unspecified completeness calls for a precise limitation, not guessed values. Known failures take precedence over uncertainty in the same check. Null is reserved for an entirely unassessable criterion, never known failure or numerical inapplicability.

AUDIT IN THIS ORDER; OUTPUT ONLY THE DECLARED FIELDS
1. Q WITHOUT S: State the real subject, known context, final unknown and answer type. Ignore annotations as possible repairs. A named class, explicit fragment or operational description can identify the subject at the requested level; a local label cannot supply missing connectivity, a material-specific stage or unnamed apparatus. Broad but usable is different from unidentifiable. Necessary terminology, simple functions and one-step procedures are not defects.
2. SCOPE IN S: Verify Q's settled premises and roles using the whole permitted source. Preserve subject/embodiment, actor, location, direction, causal attribution, quantifiers, optionality and evidence status. In assertion checking, isolate the uncertain proposition from settled premises; do not assume it is true. Do not silently repair an apparent source typo or exaggerate a proposed use into measured efficacy.
3. A AND E COVERAGE: Identify each material requested component and locate its deciding content in A and E. A correct E cannot repair a shorter incomplete A. Full-source relevance cannot repair omitted selected content. Ask for the missing link, not just matching nouns: identity plus variable definitions, both comparison sides plus their asked relation, every needed route dependency, or each source-finding clue feature. Explicit referent resolution is allowed; adding absent properties, conditions or operations is not. Evidence of existence can answer existence, not contents. A complete answer need not be an executable protocol.
4. LOGIC AND QUANTITIES: Match each quantity to its subject/stage, variable, unit, denominator, bounds and relation, including quantities in Q. Equal allowed ranges do not prove equal realized values; A-or-B does not establish A alone; required is not optional. One possible response may use one permitted branch, whereas a broad response request must preserve the alternatives. Audit immediately adjacent provisos and required endpoint operations. Different-stage values may both be valid. A same-subject conflict is unresolved unless the supplied text establishes how it is resolved; later/cleaner/preferred text does not automatically win.
5. REPRESENTATION: Compare E against S, A against E under the translation contract, annotations against Q, and identity against metadata. Do not compare free Q wording against S as though it must be copied. Trust caller-supplied deterministic checks only for their stated field/version/normalization; neither semantic confidence nor a polished answer establishes literal equality. Then check the annotation's meaning. Apply only the required fields for this persona, not empty columns belonging to other modes.
6. ASSIGN AND RECONCILE: Identify each failure's owner and affected criterion before choosing scores. Check scores, reasons and statuses for contradictions. Do not penalize unaffected dimensions to lower a total or invent an extra failure to justify a rejection. Do not assume that an earlier stage already performed these checks.

THREE DIFFERENT FAILURE OWNERS
MODE: actual information need, independently intelligible Q, declared question language and prohibited locators. A bad answer does not by itself change a well-formed question's persona. An unanchored Q can still have a factually supported answer to its source-local referent.
SUPPORT: substantive truth of settled premises, complete answer/evidence coverage and matching scope, including a submitted assertion verdict or source identity. An unidentifiable requested material in A is an answer failure, not just weak wording. Known nonresolution is fail, not a review-worthy maybe. A genuinely unresolved source conflict or genuinely missing decisive input is uncertain unless there is already an independently established failure.
METADATA: required fields/types/enums, meaningful exact question annotations, extraction and translation flags, identity representation. A valid-enum but substantively wrong assertion verdict fails support; it need not fail metadata solely for being false. A wrong publication identity affects both provenance support and metadata. A wrong anchor string alone does not fail mode. Meaning-preserving paraphrase, whitespace or conjunction changes can fail metadata with support passing; an altered unit, connector or omission that changes the answer also affects support.
No status cancels another: each check fails if any required subcheck is known to fail; otherwise it is uncertain if a required part is unassessable; otherwise pass. A summary comment from another model is not a deterministic check or a missing original field.

BOUNDARIES THAT PREVENT FALSE POSITIVES AND FALSE REJECTIONS
- Necessary adjacent sentences, source fragments, numbering, readable serialized tables and text between indispensable clauses are permitted. Table limits need their element/header/unit associations. To deduct for answer excess, identify a shorter sufficient CONTIGUOUS span by its endpoints; never demand stitching. To deduct focus/economy, quote redundant Q wording, not a long A.
- A singular-effect question may be answered by one directly attributed effect without enumerating all downstream consequences; preserve optionality and do not use a narrow quote to silently narrow a broader Q.
- A candidate can be an intelligible class or described architecture, not necessarily a proper name. Missing scaffolds matter only for needs dependent on them. An explicit -NHR fragment may define a variable-level question; organosilane alone cannot define arbitrary X. An unknown candidate must add information beyond requirements; partial role disclosure is not complete circularity when an additional destination, control or effect remains.
- Hypothetical requirements need not describe a historical searcher, but their promised fit must be supported. Do not combine favorable attributes across candidates. Do not replace source exclusions with general scientific impossibility. Do not infer a mechanism from a specification or an isolated component's efficacy from a combined-system effect.
- Literal substring checks are case-sensitive and use the final Q, including exact inflection and intervening characters. X1至X3 is in X1至X3的可选基团; ein Bild is not in einem Bild. A when/wenn clause can be a valid constraint substring. Accents or spelling in a free question need not match the source. Do not invent source-copied anchors or normalize missing characters in your head.
- Q cannot use the sought publication number or exact full title as a shortcut. An indispensable unseen-document pointer fails standalone mode; a dispensable this-process pointer in an otherwise independently identified question can be a nonfatal wording limitation. Neutral reported/proposed wording preserves evidence status. Unnecessary drawing numbers do not supply context and are not scientific falsehoods by themselves.
- No obligatory year, chemical-name synonym quota, question word-count limit, exceptional-score quota, novelty requirement, imagined retrieval ranking or corpus-wide uniqueness requirement. Preserve meaningful technical names. Do not penalize an ordinary binder role merely as familiar. A device/process in the chemical corpus is not excluded solely for being an apparatus; do not invent a new subject filter.

PERSONA CONTRACT
One technically meaningful proposition is explicitly uncertain. Its evidence relation is unknown. An open what/how question with no proposition does not become assertion checking through a supported annotation. A question such as can X do Y? contains a proposition but still needs the correct scope.
First identify the entire assertion from the QUESTION, including connectors, subject, quantifiers and attribution. Only then compare the independently determined relation with assertion_status. SUPPORTED requires all material parts. CONTRADICTED requires explicit incompatible evidence at the same scope; an in-scope counterexample can refute a universal. Silence, partial support, missing experiments and a narrower statement are not contradiction. An uncertain false assertion correctly refuted is valid; its deliberately wrong number is not itself numerical infidelity.
A disclosure exclusion does not establish physical impossibility outside that disclosure. A stated operating interval does not establish inactivity outside it. Reduced alone does not contradict eliminated. Permitted alternatives do not prove which option was realized. Preserve and versus or and required versus optional.
Explicit method-existence language can support a preparation-existence assertion for an identifiable class without experimental steps. It cannot answer a request for the steps. A supported assertion can be useful; do not require false assertions, a dispute story, numerical mutations or balanced verdict counts.
There is no indeterminate output label in the generator contract. With complete relevant input but undecidable evidence, a submitted categorical verdict fails support; do not replace it with the opposite label. With the decisive input explicitly unavailable, distinguish review uncertainty from a known wrong verdict. Do not treat the tested property itself as a settled identifying premise when the subject would otherwise be unidentifiable.

ABSOLUTE SCORING; NO DEFAULT TOTAL
Score each dimension independently after the audit. Do not initialize everything to four, target 32, rank the batch or force dispersion.
5 = extraordinary, with no weakness in THIS dimension and affirmative evidence beyond ordinary successful execution. Name the actual feature and why it crosses that criterion's exceptional threshold. Every quality-five note starts "Exceptional evidence:"; faithfulness uses its specified exceptional phrases. No quantity of digits, source complexity or absence of errors alone earns five.
4 = strong, complete successful execution. It can be defect-free. Cite the concrete strength; do not invent a flaw to justify four.
3 = adequate with an identifiable nonfatal limitation, or genuinely unresolved evidence ambiguity for an evidence criterion. Name the limitation; no unexplained fallback three.
2 = a material defect requiring correction. Apply the affected ceilings.
1 = the dimension's central purpose fails or the central requested content is absent.
A high unaffected score never repairs a failed check. Conversely, one failed check is not permission to lower every score. Multiple or zero justified fives are allowed.

FIVE CRITERIA — MAXIMUM 25

checking_search_realism
4: A concise, plausible checking need with a genuine uncertain proposition; either verdict can be valid.
3: The checking intent is real but mechanically or verbosely phrased.
5: Exceptionally natural checking language exposes a consequential uncertainty without manufacturing a dispute. Explain the feature with "Exceptional evidence:".
2: The actual need is the wrong persona or substantially fails the intended searcher need.
1: No usable information need of this type remains.

assertion_scope
4: The technical subject, proposition, conditions and attribution are clear from the question.
3: The proposition is usable but a nonessential aspect is broad.
5: Economical wording controls a consequential disclosure-versus-feasibility or quantifier distinction. Explain the feature with "Exceptional evidence:".
2: Essential subject, side, scope or boundary is absent, false or available only from unprovided local context.
1: No independently identifiable subject/task remains.

verdict_decisiveness
4: The submitted verdict is established by sufficient matching-scope evidence for the complete proposition.
3: A real missing-input limitation prevents complete assessment, not a known unjustified verdict.
5: An exceptional alignment of proposition, label and excerpt preserves a consequential logical distinction; routine correct confirmation/refutation remains four. Explain the feature with "Exceptional evidence:".
2: A material requested component, relation, condition or applicable verdict/identity is unsupported or unresolved through a known omission.
1: The central unknown has no usable answer, or the requested answer is already wholly settled in Q.

focus_and_economy — Q ONLY
4: One coherent bounded need, with economical wording and necessary context. Multiple connected steps, a condition set or a comparison can be one need.
3: Identify genuinely removable Q scaffolding or a mildly loose answer boundary.
2: Independent bundled asks, substantial avoidable source-shaped wording or a predominantly circular request.
1: No bounded information need or the requested answer is wholly supplied as settled context.
5: Exceptional wording gives the need an unusually precise boundary while preserving scope; identify the exact design choice beyond merely one question mark or short length.
Never use A's length, necessary evidence bridges, a wrong persona by itself or a metadata fault as the reason for this score.

linguistic_quality — Q'S ACTUAL LANGUAGE
4: Correct, clear, natural technical writing; routine polished prose may be four without a flaw.
3: Name the specific awkward syntax, excessive nesting, ambiguous modifier or stilted expression and its effect. Do not invent a stylistic fault to vary the batch.
2: Consequential or multiple expression/grammar problems impede reading.
1: Unintelligible or unusable prose.
5: An identifiable editorial choice handles the actual information burden with extraordinary precision and economy; correct grammar alone is insufficient.
Chemical identity, mode, evidence omissions and annotation faults do not automatically lower language. Do not impose English syntax on other languages. Source-language extractive fragments, conventional technical names and faithfully copied Traditional Chinese are not automatically language errors.

MANDATORY STATUS/SCORE LINKS — ONLY WHERE THE STATED CAUSE APPLIES
Wrong persona -> mode=fail and checking_search_realism<=2; do not automatically lower anchoring, economy or grammar.
Essential Q identity/context absent -> mode=fail and assertion_scope<=2; supported source-local facts can still pass support.
Material false premise or known answer/evidence omission -> support=fail and verdict_decisiveness<=2; central absence -> verdict_decisiveness=1. Match affected faithfulness deductions, but evaluate independently rather than copying another grader.
Genuine source/input uncertainty with no established failure -> support=uncertain and verdict_decisiveness<=3, or null if wholly unassessable.
No actual unknown because the complete requested answer is supplied as settled context -> mode=fail, checking_search_realism<=2 and verdict_decisiveness=1. This does not make true source facts false. Known requirements, uncertain assertions, remembered source clues and useful partial route landmarks are not automatically leakage.
Copy-only/annotation-only failure -> metadata=fail, with no automatic mode/support failure or semantic score deduction. Valid literal annotation can still have the wrong meaning; explain the semantic mismatch rather than claiming a nonmatch.
A scope error does not automatically make Q wordy. An evidence-coverage failure does not make its grammar poor. An actually awkward syntactic attachment can lower language even when chemistry is correct.
