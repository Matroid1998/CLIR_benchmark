Optional caller fields include candidate_id, question_language, declared transport completeness, and narrowly scoped deterministic validation results. Do not invent them. Skips are not low-quality candidates and are handled before grading.

ORDERED AUDIT OF EACH ACTUAL CANDIDATE
1. QUESTION: Identify the final unknown, subject, scope and necessary conditions from the question alone, BEFORE looking at answer form or persona annotations. Classify that actual need, without rewriting it. Known constraints, an uncertain assertion, a remembered clue or route landmarks can leave a genuine unknown. A partially supplied role is not complete circularity if an additional destination, connection or effect remains to be learned; judge that remainder. If all the answer is already settled in the question, circularity concerns quality/mode, not automatically false source facts.
2. THREE STRINGS: Locate EVIDENCE as one exact contiguous SOURCE substring; separately compare actual ANSWER to EVIDENCE. Content-answer personas require exact equality when answer_is_translation=false; otherwise require a faithful translation of that one span. A complete evidence field cannot repair a shorter incomplete answer. Source-finding instead requires the exact target identifier as answer, with selected evidence independently covering the clue.
3. COVERAGE: Map each material unknown to the actual answer, preserving subject and quantifiers. “At least one of A or B has a gap” does not establish that A has a gap: retaining the disjunction in a quote still fails to resolve an A-only question. Both comparison sides, required operations and final-stage qualifications must be covered. For source-finding map EVERY substantive clue to SELECTED evidence. Judge semantic coverage, not matching every noun; contextual reference resolution is allowed, but cannot add an omitted technical property. Full-body relevance cannot rescue missing selected content. Known material nonresolution fails support; genuine unresolved source/input conflict is uncertain.
4. SCIENTIFIC SCOPE: Audit quantitative premises in the QUESTION as well as reproduced values in the answer: subject/stage, variable definition, value/bounds, unit, denominator and relation. Read relevant source occurrences for conflicts and status changes. A correct quote does not validate an ambiguous or changed question premise. Preserve chemical categories, qualifiers, connectors and source attribution.
5. ANNOTATIONS: Test required keys/types/enums, literal substrings against the final QUESTION, extraction fields against their proper counterpart, and identifiers against metadata. Preserve actual inflections and intervening words: “ein Bild” is not in “einem Bild”, and “agent actif” is not in “agent très actif”. Then test annotation meaning: generic “what benefit” is not a technical anchor, and a rationale label does not fit a mere role query. Broad or overlapping subtypes can have more than one defensible fit; a preferred label alone is not grounds for failure.
6. RECONCILE: Each literal-failure note must name the field pair and an actual differing/missing fragment. An inserted adjective can break evidence/source equality even when answer=evidence. Conversely, identical strings cannot support a mismatch finding. Remove a proposed failure if rechecking shows a match. Free question wording may correct source spelling or accents; it need not copy source phrasing. Fail only an actual contracted field comparison. Align notes, checks and affected scores before output; do not invent extra faults to support a rejection.

LOCAL LABELS, FRAGMENTS AND IDENTITIES
Test the question with the source hidden. A known named class, functional class, fragment or operational description can identify the subject at the asked level; incidental labels need not invalidate it. Known enzyme inhibition can anchor a disease lookup, and named protecting roles can anchor a shared-option comparison without full scaffolds. A stated hydroxy-to-alkoxy precursor relation can be intelligible without exact ring positions. But properties being tested as uncertain in an assertion cannot also be assumed as settled identifying facts. Generic “the intermediate” or “this treatment” does not identify whose material/process-specific temperature or operating range is requested. Stage labels alone do not supply an omitted alloy or treatment identity. Distinguish this essential absence from a broad but meaningful named operation; corpus-wide uniqueness is not required.
Conversely, a class name does not define an arbitrary attachment position or coefficient. Asking the permitted groups or carbon count of X “in organosilanes” requires the relevant fragment/attachment definition; unrelated component names do not define X. Asking about R in an explicitly given -NHR fragment can be meaningful at that fragment level without the whole molecule. An empirical glass index requires the formula and roles needed for the actual numerical relation. Missing essential question context is mode/anchoring failure; it does not make copied source values false. Broad but usable context or a dispensable “this process” pointer can warrant a nonfatal limitation instead.
For “which reactant/material?”, copied substituent lists around a missing parent scaffold do not supply an exact requested identity. In contrast, a recognizable class or concrete design description can identify a candidate without a proper name or every construction detail. A colored body/contrasting entry arrangement is additional device identity, not merely “this invention”. Inspect what the answer adds beyond known constraints. Distinguish missing answer identity from an unanchored question about an otherwise explicit source fact; the latter fails mode, not automatically support.

ANSWER BOUNDARIES AND PREREQUISITES
One adequate contiguous body span from one supplied unit, or its faithful translation, must resolve the actual need. Necessary adjacent sentences, original numbering, lowercase fragments and readable serialized tables are allowed. Table headers/units and row/column associations must be supplied; a table introduction alone supplies no limits. Do not invent cell values or demand new prose synthesis.
Read immediately adjacent provisos (“provided that”, “when”, “unless”) before accepting a boundary. Required reaction partners, alpha-hydrogen conditions, alternatives and qualifications cannot be cut away just because the retained clause is verbatim. A quote cannot silently narrow an overbroad question. General alternatives must stay alternatives; one explicitly requested possible response may use one branch. A list of targets does not establish an additional immobilization relationship.
For a route, trace dependencies, not only first and last quoted verbs. Correction requiring target and object coordinates needs both measurements unless the question establishes those inputs as available; beginning at a later numbered step supplies no such precondition. An explicitly preconditioned subroute can omit earlier preparation. Read the next sentence for a required final conversion: cleaning a feedstock may not yet produce the enriched finished substrate requested. Distinguish candidate-level identification from actually omitting a stage that supplies a named requirement. No executable human/laboratory protocol is required: explicit current introduction, field interaction, pressure generation and circulation can be operating steps connecting a medium to temperature regulation. A single stated transformation is also procedural; a method name or ingredient inventory without the requested action is not.
For a singular effect, one directly attributed effect can suffice without every downstream consequence. A functional noun can state a role without a mechanism. Asking the role of independently named P is supported; asking the function of “the inhibitor” may give it away. Check whether a destination, port connection or further stated effect still answers a real remainder before treating partial role leakage as complete circularity. Familiarity is not a defect.
To penalize answer padding, locate a shorter sufficient CONTIGUOUS span with both endpoints. Intervening text between required comparison or structure-to-effect clauses is a necessary bridge. A later sentence can define how the named method achieves its goal: spectral-channel routing remains relevant to spectrally selective imaging even after text mentions image formation. Do not shorten by stitching, changing scope or stripping optional-presence/subject qualifiers. A harmless conjunction is not meaningful excess. Question focus_and_economy assesses QUESTION wording only, never answer length; missing context or wrong persona alone does not prove redundant wording.

QUANTITIES, RELATIONS AND SOURCE CONFLICTS
Audit the question's denominator explicitly. “Wastewater at up to 40 wt% of the hourly salt-stream mass” does not mean “40 wt% of the wastewater”; omitting the denominator can leave a material ambiguity even when the answer retains it. Likewise polymer mass is not wet-dispersion mass, and active-material content is not total-formulation content. Preserve units/exponents; plausible source typos are not permission to fix them. If chemical terminology itself is odd or ambiguous, identify that limitation rather than endorse an unstated correction. Unsaturated heterocycle does not automatically mean heteroaryl; precursor does not mean product.
Equal allowed ranges/options do not prove equal realized values or choices. Explicit “same permitted linkage options?” can be resolved when both components allow oxygen OR carbon attachment. Explicit “actually use the same linkage?” cannot be established from those independent alternatives. Ambiguous “same linkage type?” can reasonably concern permitted options: state the wording ambiguity and assess its materiality; neither silently rewrite it nor invent an unequivocal actual-identity claim. Matching bounds alone cannot support an explicit actual-equality assertion.
Compare global restrictions with local preferred/example ranges at the SAME subject and conditions. A general pH<6 condition and a preferred pH 3–6 interval have an unresolved upper-endpoint issue unless the supplied text explains it. Do not automatically let “preferred” override the general rule, silently remove the endpoint, or declare the copied local range wholly verified. Different stages or embodiments may legitimately differ; state a concrete same-scope conflict rather than manufacture one.
Optional is not required, preferred is not exclusive, examples are not exhaustive, and intended/observed effects do not establish an unstated mechanism. A patent restriction does not establish physical impossibility outside it. Keep embodiment, example and attribution scope.

ASSERTIONS, SELECTION AND SOURCE FINDING
Determine an assertion's uncertain proposition independently of assertion_status: supported, explicitly contradicted or not decidable. The contract permits only supported/contradicted; a forced verdict on an indeterminate claim fails support. Silence is not contradiction; “reduced” alone does not refute “eliminated”. A false proposition posed as uncertain and correctly contradicted is valid. Preserve connectors and quantifiers: an existential alternative may need one branch, a conjunction needs every material conjunct; never change “and” to “and/or”. “Methods of preparing these named compounds” can establish method EXISTENCE when source context resolves the class. It supplies no operations for a route request; a class-level existence claim needs no irrelevant missing scaffold.
Constraint search requires an objective and meaningful requirement selecting an unknown candidate; one substrate/feedstock/plant-origin restriction can suffice, and multiple candidates are allowed. “Which process makes blocks from polluted soil within a firing-temperature window?” selects an approach even if its answer contains eight stages. Classify the QUESTION, not answer length or numbering. A complete operational description can identify the approach; it need not have a proper name. Conversely, “which gas does this furnace produce/reuse?” is identity lookup, since provenance/destination alone do not request selection. Explicitly asking ordered operations is process retrieval.
Source-finding has known technical clue and unknown publication identity. Check the exact target number against metadata (it need not appear in the body), and all clue content against selected body evidence. True but unidentifiable clues can fail mode/anchoring; false clues fail support. Do not require firstness, legal status or corpus-wide uniqueness.

LITERAL INTEGRITY AND SEMANTIC SUPPORT
A faithful same-language paraphrase, benign spacing normalization or “und” changed to “sowie” can fail extraction metadata while preserving scientific support. Do not label all such evidence fabricated or automatically lower semantic precision/grounding/language. Changed values, deleted alternatives, substantive omissions, reordered operations and mixed stages affect meaning as well. Compare actual strings and their meaning separately. Traditional Chinese copied faithfully from Chinese source text is not automatically bad language; free question wording follows its declared language. Faithfulness reports representation-only problems in its reason; quality reports them under metadata. Neither branch should invent a semantic penalty for them.

ABSOLUTE SCORE LADDER — NO 32-POINT DEFAULT
Judge each criterion from its own evidence. Never begin by assigning every dimension four and look only for obvious hallucinations. Three, four and five are not ranks or batch percentiles.
5: Extraordinary, with no weakness in that dimension AND concrete positive evidence beyond routine success. Every quality-five note begins “Exceptional evidence:”; faithfulness reasons use “Grounding exceptional:”, “Precision exceptional:” or “Numerical fidelity exceptional:”. Name what is exceptional, not simply that the answer is correct, concise or fluent.
4: Strong, fully successful execution; no defect is needed. Cite the actual strength, not a generic “all criteria met”.
3: Adequate with a concrete nonfatal limitation or, for evidence criteria, a genuinely unresolved evidential ambiguity. State which. Do not use vague “could be better” or invent limitations to spread scores.
2: A major defect in this dimension; apply mandatory ceilings.
1: The dimension's central purpose fails or required central content is absent.
Do not require fives, forbid fives, create a low-score quota or promote the best candidate in a weak batch. Simple questions, ordinary roles, necessary shared terminology, nonnumeric content and long necessary evidence can all be strong. Technical complexity, rare vocabulary, social importance, imagined retrieval rankings and low lexical overlap earn no intrinsic credit. Scoring an unaffected dimension highly never cures a failed validity check elsewhere.


PERSONA CONTRACT
ASSERTION CHECKING — uncertain proposition, unknown evidence relation
A concise plausible assertion is explicitly uncertain. supported requires every material part at the stated subject, embodiment, conditions and attribution; contradicted requires explicit incompatible evidence at that SAME scope. Silence, partial evidence, missing structure or a missing experiment is not contradiction. “Reduced” alone does not refute “eliminated”. A disclosed operating range does not prove inactivity outside it. An exclusion from one formulation does not refute general physical or therapeutic feasibility. Prefer a precise disclosure-level assertion when that is all the packet supports; an existential “has a combination excluding X been disclosed?” avoids claiming all combinations exclude X. Do not fix a missing scope by inserting an unseen-document pointer. A supported assertion can be a real uncertainty. An explicit statement that methods of preparing a named class are disclosed can settle an existence claim without detailing steps or a full scaffold; it cannot settle an operations request. No preference for false assertions, random numerical changes or verdict balance. answer is the decisive excerpt, not a bare verdict or added correction. assertion_status is supported or contradicted; discard indeterminate assertions.

ANNOTATION CONTRACT
answer is the deciding excerpt, not a bare yes/no or an added correction. assertion is the exact question substring expressing the uncertain claim; surrounding wording may supply scope. assertion_status is supported or contradicted. The complete question without its final question mark may be used when the assertion is expressed through interrogative grammar.
anchor must occur exactly in question and identify technical content; generic “benefit is reported” alone is insufficient. Other required question substrings must be exact and meaningful; shorter real substrings are allowed. Check subtype meaning, but do not fail a defensible broad/overlapping label just because another seems preferable. Compare source_identifier to target metadata; never reconstruct unseen fields.

CHECKS: mode, support, metadata
mode: pass only when the actual final ask belongs to this persona AND its standalone subject, question-language and locator contract are satisfied. Essential missing scaffold/context is mode=fail even when the quotation's facts are true. A useful question in another persona is not valid under this one. Do not disqualify a simple explicit function, a shared comparison, a real one-requirement search, or an explicit one-step transformation just for being simple.
support: pass only when the submitted answer and available original evidence establish the actual settled premises and complete unknown at matching scope/status, including an applicable verdict/identity. Full-body relevance cannot repair omitted selected answer content. Known material failures are fail, not uncertain. A genuinely unresolved same-subject source conflict or missing decisive input can be uncertain; explain exactly what is unresolved. For a submitted categorical assertion label, known inability to establish that label is a failed verdict, not permission to invent its opposite. If original source-finding evidence or assertion verdict is omitted from a review export and cannot be audited, do not claim complete support for the unseen object.
metadata: check required fields/types, enum membership AND substantive annotation meaning, supplied translation flags, exact question substrings, and evidence/source integrity. For nontranslated content answers also compare answer/evidence; for source_finding compare answer/source_identifier to target metadata. A mismatch note must identify the correct field pair and the actual difference, not merely assert one: “X1至X3” does occur in “X1至X3的可选基团”. If all compared characters match, remove that failure; different free question/source spelling is not an extraction defect. A generic anchor or wrong subtype can fail meaning despite exact occurrence/valid enum. Meaning-preserving copy faults can be metadata=fail with support=pass; wrong target identity or verdict also fails support. Missing raw fields fail, explicitly unavailable transport fields are uncertain. Do not claim checks on unseen strings.

MANDATORY CEILINGS, APPLIED ONLY TO AFFECTED CRITERIA
- Wrong persona: mode=fail; checking_search_realism <=2. Do not automatically reduce valid technical anchoring, economy or grammar.
- Essential standalone context absent or supplied only by an undefined local label: mode=fail; assertion_scope <=2, or one if no identifiable subject remains. Broad but usable context with a concrete nonessential weakness can be three. Do not require corpus-wide uniqueness.
- A material unsupported premise, omitted requested side/step/condition, wrong verdict, false source clue or unidentifiable requested candidate: support=fail; verdict_decisiveness <=2, or one when its central requested content is absent. The faithfulness counterpart must also lower affected grounding/precision; do not pretend that copying alone is enough.
- If a real source/input ambiguity makes semantic resolution genuinely uncertain, support=uncertain and verdict_decisiveness <=3; no strong four for an unresolved answer. Do not lower numerical or language scores for unrelated ambiguity.
- The requested answer completely supplied as settled background: verdict_decisiveness=1. Partial disclosure that makes the remaining need trivial or circular limits the relevant intent/resolution score. Known constraints, uncertain assertions, remembered clues, or selected known route features are not automatically leakage; locate what actually remains unknown.
- A literal-only mismatch or wrong substring annotation: metadata fails/needs review as established; no automatic support failure or deduction from semantic resolution, intent or language. The exact copy contract still needs repair.
- Multiple independent asks or avoidable long source-shaped scaffolding lower focus_and_economy. Name removable QUESTION wording. Do not penalize necessary technical terms or the extractive answer for being copied. Essential qualifications and necessary evidence bridges are not padding.
Scores and checks are not alternatives. If assertion_scope=2 because a scaffold is essential and absent, mode cannot be pass. If the answer is known not to resolve a material relation, support cannot be pass. A high total never overrides a failed check. Conversely, do not lower unaffected dimensions to engineer a low total.

FIVE CRITERIA
CHECKING_SEARCH_REALISM
5: Exemplar-level formulation of a meaningful uncertainty, avoiding a subtle scope trap through particularly natural and precise checking language.
4: A plausible, concise scoped assertion whose evidence status is genuinely unknown. Supported and contradicted outcomes are equally valid.
3: Real checking intent but noticeably mechanical or verbose, with a concrete nonfatal weakness.
2: Arbitrary number mutation, paragraph-wrapper checking, or a different persona's unknown.
1: No coherent uncertain assertion. A proposed false value is not automatically a hallucinated settled premise.

ASSERTION_SCOPE
5: Exceptionally precise and economical assertion scope that controls a consequential subject/embodiment/status distinction without source-local locators.
4: The technical subject, claim scope and attribution are clear in the question itself.
3: A usable but broad assertion with a nonessential ambiguity.
2: Essential identity/scope is missing or a general physical claim is being substituted for a disclosure-specific restriction.
1: An unidentifiable or unbounded proposition. A narrow quote cannot retroactively scope a broad question.

VERDICT_DECISIVENESS
5: Exceptional alignment of the entire actual uncertain assertion, its correct verdict and decisive contiguous evidence, preserving a consequential logical distinction beyond routine confirmation/refutation.
4: The submitted verdict is decisively supported by matching-scope evidence; no claim of independent scientific truth.
3: Genuine missing-input ambiguity needs review; do not call a known unjustified verdict adequate.
2: Wrong or forced verdict, incomplete deciding scope or a claimed contradiction based on silence, a narrower rule, or general feasibility inferred from a particular exclusion.
1: No decisive evidence for the central proposition, invented verdict basis or unresolved candidate identity essential to the check. A meaning-preserving quotation-copy defect alone must not lower this semantic decisiveness score.

FOCUS_AND_ECONOMY
5: Exceptionally precise control of one real information need: every necessary contextual element and subpart does distinct work, with an unusually effective boundary that avoids circularity without sacrificing technical scope. Name the wording that achieves this; shortness or one question mark alone is not enough.
4: One coherent, clearly bounded need, economical natural wording and necessary terminology retained. No flaw is required.
3: Usable intent with identifiable removable QUESTION scaffolding/redundancy or question wording that leaves the requested answer boundary mildly loose. Quote that wording; actual answer length is not this criterion. Do not assert unmeasured search-frequency assumptions.
2: Independent bundled asks, a diffuse summary request, substantial avoidable source-shaped copying or a genuinely circular remainder.
1: No coherent bounded request or an essentially reproduced answer masquerading as a question.
Grade question economy, not the length of its required evidence. A score note citing a long answer, internal crystallographic data, or unavoidable quote bridges does not justify lowering focus_and_economy. Quote the redundant words in the question itself, or retain the strong score. Multiple connected steps, two comparison sides and a coherent set of limits can serve one need. A wrong persona alone does not establish redundant wording.

LINGUISTIC_QUALITY
5: Extraordinary editorial precision in the actual language, with a concrete phrasing choice that preserves a difficult distinction or information burden exceptionally clearly and economically. Routine fluency is not automatically five; technical difficulty is not a bonus.
4: Correct, natural, clear and appropriately technical wording. A fully polished sentence can be four without any defect.
3: Understandable but specifically stiff, ambiguous in expression, redundant or awkward; identify the actual language issue rather than a factual disagreement.
2: Substantial grammar, expression or mixed-language problems impede interpretation.
1: Unintelligible or unusable question language.
Do not lower this score just because the science, mode, metadata, source identity or answer completeness is wrong. “Grammatically sound but missing the chemical scaffold” primarily lowers anchoring/mode, not grammar. Judge authentic norms in all five languages, not English word counts or unexplained preference for a spelling variant. Preserve allowed extractive fragments and technical names.

Explain each score using actual wording or source evidence, not a boilerplate compliment; every five-note starts “Exceptional evidence:”. Every fail/uncertain check has an evidence-based problems entry prefixed mode:, support: or metadata:. Do not invent an additional reason to reinforce a justified rejection. Metadata-only repairs must not be called false chemistry.
A single candidate has batch_diversity=not_applicable. For multiple candidates, assess duplicate INFORMATION NEEDS only; no quota for categories or verdicts, no individual score changes for diversity. Reverse comparison order or opposite assertion polarity is not automatically a new need. Across different patents or languages, do not mistake parallel translations for within-document candidate duplicates; use the actual batch task.
Use null only for a genuinely unassessable entire criterion. Return no total, overall grade, ranking, rewrite or reasoning trace. Complete the final reconciliation after the examples; known errors, notes, checks and affected ceilings must agree. Do not invent additional faults to justify a rejection.
