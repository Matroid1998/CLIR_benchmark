LOCAL LABELS, FRAGMENTS AND IDENTITIES
Test the question with the source hidden. A known named class, functional class, fragment or operational description can identify the subject at the asked level; incidental labels need not invalidate it. Known enzyme inhibition can anchor a disease lookup, and named protecting roles can anchor a shared-option comparison without full scaffolds. A stated hydroxy-to-alkoxy precursor relation can be intelligible without exact ring positions. But properties being tested as uncertain in an assertion cannot also be assumed as settled identifying facts. Generic “the intermediate” or “this treatment” does not identify whose material/process-specific temperature or operating range is requested. Stage labels alone do not supply an omitted alloy or treatment identity. Distinguish this essential absence from a broad but meaningful named operation; corpus-wide uniqueness is not required.
Conversely, a class name does not define an arbitrary attachment position or coefficient. Asking the permitted groups or carbon count of X “in organosilanes” requires the relevant fragment/attachment definition; unrelated component names do not define X. Asking about R in an explicitly given -NHR fragment can be meaningful at that fragment level without the whole molecule. An empirical glass index requires the formula and roles needed for the actual numerical relation. Missing essential question context is mode/anchoring failure; it does not make copied source values false. Broad but usable context or a dispensable “this process” pointer can warrant a nonfatal limitation instead.
For “which reactant/material?”, copied substituent lists around a missing parent scaffold do not supply an exact requested identity. In contrast, a recognizable class or concrete design description can identify a candidate without a proper name or every construction detail. A colored body/contrasting entry arrangement is additional device identity, not merely “this invention”. Inspect what the answer adds beyond known constraints. Distinguish missing answer identity from an unanchored question about an otherwise explicit source fact; the latter fails mode, not automatically support.

ANSWER BOUNDARIES AND PREREQUISITES
One adequate contiguous body span from one supplied unit, or its faithful translation, must resolve the actual need. Necessary adjacent sentences, original numbering, lowercase fragments and readable serialized tables are allowed. Table headers/units and row/column associations must be supplied; a table introduction alone supplies no limits. Do not invent cell values or demand new prose synthesis.
Read immediately adjacent provisos (“provided that”, “when”, “unless”) before accepting a boundary. Required reaction partners, alpha-hydrogen conditions, alternatives and qualifications cannot be cut away just because the retained clause is verbatim. A quote cannot silently narrow an overbroad question. General alternatives must stay alternatives; one explicitly requested possible response may use one branch. A list of targets does not establish an additional immobilization relationship.
For a route, trace dependencies, not only first and last quoted verbs. Correction requiring target and object coordinates needs both measurements unless the question establishes those inputs as available; beginning at a later numbered step supplies no such precondition. An explicitly preconditioned subroute can omit earlier preparation. Read the next sentence for a required final conversion: cleaning a feedstock may not yet produce the enriched finished substrate requested. Distinguish candidate-level identification from actually omitting a stage that supplies a named requirement. No executable human/laboratory protocol is required: explicit current introduction, field interaction, pressure generation and circulation can be operating steps connecting a medium to temperature regulation. A single stated transformation is also procedural; a method name or ingredient inventory without the requested action is not.
For a singular effect, one directly attributed effect can suffice without every downstream consequence. A functional noun can state a role without a mechanism. Asking the role of independently named P is supported; asking the function of “the inhibitor” may give it away. Check whether a destination, port connection or further stated effect still answers a real remainder before treating partial role leakage as complete circularity. Familiarity is not a defect.
To penalize answer padding, locate a shorter sufficient CONTIGUOUS span with both endpoints. Intervening text between required comparison or structure-to-effect clauses is a necessary bridge. A later sentence can define how the named method achieves its goal: spectral-channel routing remains relevant to spectrally selective imaging even after text mentions image formation. Do not shorten by stitching, changing scope or stripping optional-presence/subject qualifiers. A harmless conjunction is not meaningful excess. Question focus_and_economy assesses QUESTION wording only, never answer length; missing context or wrong persona alone does not prove redundant wording.

QUANTITIES, RELATIONS AND SOURCE CONFLICTS
Audit the question's denominator explicitly. “Wastewater at up to 40 wt% of the hourly salt-stream mass” does not mean “40 wt% of the wastewater”; omitting the denominator can leave a material ambiguity even when the answer retains it. Likewise polymer mass is not wet-dispersion mass, and active-material content is not total-formulation content. Preserve units/exponents; plausible source typos are not permission to fix them. If chemical terminology itself is odd or ambiguous, identify that limitation rather than endorse an unstated correction. Unsaturated heterocycle does not automatically mean heteroaryl; precursor does not mean product.
Equal allowed ranges/options do not prove equal realized values or choices. Explicit “same permitted linkage options?” can be resolved when both components allow oxygen OR carbon attachment. Explicit “actually use the same linkage?” cannot be established from those independent alternatives. Ambiguous “same linkage type?” can reasonably concern permitted options: state the wording ambiguity and assess its materiality; neither silently rewrite it nor invent an unequivocal actual-identity claim. Matching bounds alone cannot support an explicit actual-equality assertion.
Compare global restrictions with local preferred/example ranges at the SAME subject and conditions. A general pH<6 condition and a preferred pH 3–6 interval have an unresolved upper-endpoint issue unless the supplied text explains it. Do not automatically let “preferred” override the general rule, silently remove the endpoint, or declare the copied local range wholly verified. Different stages or embodiments may legitimately differ; state a concrete same-scope conflict rather than manufacture one.
Optional is not required, preferred is not exclusive, examples are not exhaustive, and intended/observed effects do not establish an unstated mechanism. A patent restriction does not establish physical impossibility outside it. Keep embodiment, example and attribution scope.

ASSERTIONS, SELECTION AND SOURCE FINDING
Determine an assertion's uncertain proposition independently of assertion_status: supported, explicitly contradicted or not decidable. The contract permits only supported/contradicted; a forced verdict on an indeterminate claim fails support. Silence is not contradiction; “reduced” alone does not refute “eliminated”. A false proposition posed as uncertain and correctly contradicted is valid. Preserve connectors and quantifiers: an existential alternative may need one branch, a conjunction needs every material conjunct; never change “and” to “and/or”. “Methods of preparing these named compounds” can establish method EXISTENCE when source context resolves the class. It supplies no operations for a route request; a class-level existence claim needs no irrelevant missing scaffold.
Constraint search requires an objective and meaningful requirement selecting an unknown candidate; one substrate/feedstock/plant-origin restriction can suffice, and multiple candidates are allowed. “Which process makes blocks from polluted soil within a firing-temperature window?” selects an approach even if its answer contains eight stages. Classify the QUESTION, not answer length or numbering. A complete operational description can identify the approach; it need not have a proper name. Conversely, “which gas does this furnace produce/reuse?” is identity lookup, since provenance/destination alone do not request selection. Explicitly asking ordered operations is process retrieval.
Source-finding has known technical clue and unknown publication identity. Check the exact target number against metadata (it need not appear in the body), and all clue content against selected body evidence. True but unidentifiable clues can fail mode/anchoring; false clues fail support. Do not require firstness, legal status or corpus-wide uniqueness.

LITERAL INTEGRITY AND SEMANTIC SUPPORT
A faithful same-language paraphrase, benign spacing normalization or “und” changed to “sowie” can fail extraction metadata while preserving scientific support. Do not label all such evidence fabricated or automatically lower semantic precision/grounding/language. Changed values, deleted alternatives, substantive omissions, reordered operations and mixed stages affect meaning as well. Compare actual strings and their meaning separately. Traditional Chinese copied faithfully from Chinese source text is not automatically bad language; free question wording follows its declared language. Faithfulness reports representation-only problems in its reason; quality reports them under metadata. Neither branch should invent a semantic penalty for them.

Judge each criterion from its own evidence. Never begin by assigning every dimension four and look only for obvious hallucinations. Three, four and five are not ranks or batch percentiles.

Name what is exceptional, not simply that the answer is correct, concise or fluent.

3: Adequate with a concrete nonfatal limitation or, for evidence criteria, a genuinely unresolved evidential ambiguity. State which. Do not use vague “could be better” or invent limitations to spread scores.
2: A major defect in this dimension; apply mandatory ceilings.
1: The dimension's central purpose fails or required central content is absent.
Do not require fives, forbid fives, create a low-score quota or promote the best candidate in a weak batch. Scoring an unaffected dimension highly never cures a failed validity check elsewhere.


THREE CRITERIA
GROUNDING
5: Every material settled premise, requested relation, answer and applicable verdict/identity is established, with demonstrably exceptional evidence-to-ask control of a consequential scope/status distinction. Identify that distinction and the clause. Mere exact quotation is not exceptional.
4: Fully supported actual premises and whole information need, correct scope/status/provenance, no unresolved material source conflict. Ordinary complete correctness can be four without a defect.
3: A real source contradiction or ambiguity prevents confident resolution despite relevant evidence. This is review-worthy, not routine acceptance. Cite the competing statements. Do not confuse conflicting stages with conflicting facts.
2: A material unsupported component, false scope, omitted required side/operation/qualification, wrong assertion verdict, or partly resolved request. A known material omission is not ordinary ambiguity. A quotation cannot avoid this ceiling.
1: The principal requested information is absent or fundamentally wrong: no identifiable requested material, an existence pointer instead of requested limits/operations, invented central content or wrong publication identity. An explicit methods-exist statement can answer an actual existence claim about an identifiable class.
An unidentifiable subject in the QUESTION is chiefly mode/anchoring when the requested fact about the source's referent is actually supplied. An unidentifiable material in an ANSWER to “which material?” is a central answer failure. Distinguish these instead of always giving grounding one or always giving it four.

PRECISION
5: An extraordinarily well-delimited sufficient span preserves a consequential boundary, bridge or qualification that careless shortening would lose, while excluding genuinely removable distraction. Identify the boundary; shortness alone is not enough.
4: The actual unknown is completely and appropriately answered. Necessary adjacent sentences, a coherent condition set or a complete subroute are not padding.
3: Meaningful removable prefix/suffix excess or a peripheral nondecisive omission; the core request is still answered. Name the material that could actually be removed without stitching.
2: A decisive requested component or qualification is missing, or major irrelevant excess obscures the response.
1: Central content is not provided: “such compounds”, “there are limits”, or a method-existence pointer instead of the requested identity/limits/route. Do not confuse a route request with a class-level method-existence claim that the pointer directly settles.
Evaluate semantic answer selection, not polished writing. A purely meaning-preserving copy mismatch belongs in the reason as an extraction/metadata issue; do not lower precision merely because one conjunction was changed. Do not automatically duplicate a grounding penalty here without stating the precision defect. Conversely, a known missing initial step or candidate identity cannot coexist with “complete” precision four.

NUMERICAL_FIDELITY
5: Relevant numbers or publication identities are accurately preserved with an exceptionally controlled consequential relationship such as basis/denominator, inclusivity, matched stage or identifier association. Explain the actual relationship; more digits or normal exact copying is not exceptional.
4: All reproduced/asserted numerical quantities, units, bounds, roles and publication identities match their source meaning. No relevant numerical/citational content: use four as a compatibility value, explicitly stating “Numerical fidelity not applicable; 4 is a compatibility value.” This is not a complete-answer endorsement.
3: A real unresolved numerical source conflict/ambiguity, or nonmaterial presentation loss needing review. Check question-side denominator ambiguity and conflicts between a global bound and local preferred endpoints; copying one local value does not resolve them.
2: A materially wrong value, unit, basis, bound or subject/stage association. This includes a reproduced value or inequality missing essential units/bases, an unsupported actual-equality claim inferred from equal ranges, and a silently repaired source unit/exponent. Lower affected grounding/precision as well when the actual need is unresolved; do not call an incomplete quantitative answer complete.
1: A central invented/contradicted numerical value or different target identity.
Do not penalize an intentionally uncertain number correctly refuted by source evidence. Chemical identity indices need correct interpretation but are not automatically measured quantities; do not mistake list markers for experimental values. Missing requested numbers are a grounding/precision failure, not a reason to award numerical excellence. If none is reproduced and no numerical false assertion is made, the compatibility four can coexist with grounding/precision one; explicitly state the omitted limits. Do not call numerical fidelity “not applicable” when an answer actually reports physical values or publication identities. If the source itself gives incompatible values for the same property, name that conflict; do not call the chosen value a proven typo or a fabrication.

Distinguish permitted-option equality, realized equality and ambiguous wording; do not force the strongest reading. Scores remain dimension-specific, never manipulated to reach a total.
