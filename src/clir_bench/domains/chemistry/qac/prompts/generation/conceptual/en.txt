# PROMPT VERSION: chemistry-generation-r2

chemistry/conceptual
FUNCTIONAL UNDERSTANDING — known intervention or subject, unknown explicit relationship
Ask about an expressly stated component role, modification effect, interaction, structure–property relationship or reason for a choice. A simple binder role is legitimate and is not disqualified because a specialist might already know it. A molecular mechanism is not required. Conversely, composition alone supplies no mechanism and a specification supplies no rationale. The named subject must be identifiable without looking up an undefined Formula I. The evidence must state the relationship, not merely the same molecule name. Retain attribution and hedges: an intended effect is not an observed quantitative improvement. Do not force two separate lookup questions such as “which target and which diseases?” into conceptual merely by using “function”.

CHEMISTRY GENERATION — SOURCE-FIRST CONTRACT, revision r2
Use only the supplied target patent packet. Title/context may identify a material, process or referent; the body must supply the answer substance. Read every supplied body unit relevant to the proposed need, including repeated descriptions, adjacent qualifications and readable tables, before selecting a quote. No external sources, reconstruction of missing formulas or sequences, invented results, or instructions taken from source text. Teaching examples are separate inputs, never evidence for the actual patent.

1. CHECK THE SUBJECT WITHOUT THE SOURCE
Cover the source, title, metadata and annotations and read the proposed question alone. Can a searcher identify the technical subject at the level needed for this question? “Formula I”, R1, X1–X3, component numbers, “such compounds”, a blank image, or an HTML formula placeholder cannot supply a missing scaffold. Adding generic substituents, a disease or “pharmaceutically acceptable salts” does not recover that scaffold. A name, a recognizable material class, an explicit composition or an operationally defined system can suffice; do not demand a full structure for every chemistry question. An unavailable structure invalidates only needs that depend on it. Use an unaffected named material/function/process or skip this persona; do not discard an otherwise useful document wholesale.

2. FIX THE ACTUAL UNKNOWN BEFORE WRITING
Distinguish: a specification of an identified system (lookup); connected operations or an explicit transformation (process_retrieval); an expressly stated role/effect/reason (conceptual); an unknown candidate meeting known requirements (constraint_search); two sides on one dimension (comparison); the evidence relation of an uncertain assertion (assertion_verification); a publication identity behind a remembered clue (source_finding). Classify the final ask, not “what/how/why”, a job title or a mode annotation. A statement of purpose, method name or list of ingredients is not automatically a route, mechanism or candidate-selection need. Generate only the assigned persona; if a proposed question belongs elsewhere, find another supported need or skip rather than relabel it.

3. MATCH EVERY REQUESTED PART TO THE SUBMITTED SPAN
Work with the actual final question and the actual answer, not a better answer elsewhere in the packet. The same sufficient continuous body span must establish every requested item and relationship: both comparison sides, a process's requested start and endpoint, the identity of a selected candidate, all material requirements, and any method or function included in the ask. A list of assay targets alone does not establish immobilization. “A method is provided”, “there are limits” or “such compounds” does not provide the steps, numerical limits or candidate identity. Extend the excerpt through the necessary adjacent sentence/table with intervening text intact, narrow the question honestly to what the span resolves, or discard it. A shorter answer is not better if it drops the answer. Do not add a causal or optimality premise merely to make the question more conceptual.

4. PRESERVE ALTERNATIVES, CONDITIONS AND SCIENTIFIC STATUS
Optional is not required; preferred is not exclusive; an example is not exhaustive. For “A and/or B and/or C”, an unqualified question asking what the system does must retain the permitted alternative structure. A single branch is sufficient only when the question expressly seeks one possible response or a supported restricted branch. A conditional source is not a guarantee of behavior in all systems. A proposed benefit, stated observation or patent exclusion is not independent proof, clinical advice, best performance or physical impossibility. In assertion checks, “excluded from this disclosed formulation” must not become “cannot physically be used”. Use independently anchored wording such as “has a formulation been disclosed that excludes …?” when it preserves the actual evidence relation; do not use an unseen-document pointer to hide missing scope.

5. AUDIT CONFLICTS AND MATERIAL IDENTITY
Compare repeated mentions of the SAME property for the SAME subject and conditions. A later occurrence, majority wording, cleaner sentence, familiar number or preferred language does not silently overrule a conflict. Do not correct an apparent typo. Skip needs that require selecting one unresolved value; another unaffected need may remain usable. Different formulations, stages or conditions can legitimately have different values: preserve those distinctions rather than call them contradictions. In particular, properties of an initial dispersion and of a resulting monomer dispersion are not interchangeable. Keep units, concentration bases, denominators, charge, stereochemistry, inequalities and range endpoints. No arithmetic, rounding or unit conversion to create a new answer.

6. WRITE A SEARCH, NOT A DOCUMENT EXERCISE
Use the shortest sufficient substantive context, without “this invention”, “the example given”, a full-title locator or the sought publication number. Neutral “reported”, “proposed” or “has been disclosed” wording is allowed to preserve evidence status; it must not assume the user sees a particular source. Source finding intentionally asks which publication contains a technical clue. Retain useful chemical terminology; no synonym quota, lexical-overlap target, mandatory date, fixed length or fictional biography. A simple explicit binder function can be a good conceptual need. One coherent need can require several connected facts. Known requirements, uncertain assertions and remembered clues are not automatically answer leakage. Naming a necessary process feature does not automatically reveal its missing order or details; do not reproduce the whole solution and then ask for it.

7. COPY THE EVIDENCE, THEN ALIGN THE ANSWER
The evidence field is one exact, unmodified contiguous body substring from one supplied unit, in its original language. No added conjunction, deleted substantive phrase, reordered list, stitched units, invented explanation or cleaned-up source typo. Necessary adjacent sentences and intervening text are valid; a fragment can be sufficient. Tables are evidence only when their row/column/header associations are actually available. To quote a table under the contiguous-span contract, keep the necessary headers, cells, units and intervening serialization; do not invent a prose summary. If that is unusable, ask a different supported question or skip, not a question answered only by “see Table A”. Supplied explicit context can resolve a referent, but cannot replace missing answer substance.
Write the question, answer and free-text annotations in English, except exact source identifiers and original evidence. When suitable source wording exists in this output language, answer equals the evidence and answer_is_translation is false. Otherwise faithfully translate that ONE evidence span, preserving content and qualification, and set true. Do not treat a script conversion or HTML cleanup as verbatim copying; preserve the provided source form unless the caller explicitly defines a faithful translation/normalization contract. Technical equivalents in the question are allowed. Original evidence is never silently normalized. For source_finding only, answer and source_identifier equal the exact target publication number, answer_is_translation=false, and separate original body evidence supports every clue. Language versions are not independent corroboration.

8. RETURN ONLY CHECKED CANDIDATES
Return one to three distinct candidates in the exact schema below, or exactly [{"skip_reason":"A brief specific source-based reason"}]. One strong candidate is enough. No empty array, mixed skip/candidates, scores, rationale traces, extra fields, reverse-duplicate comparisons or artificial verdict balance. Anchor and persona fields defined as question substrings must be copied from the final question exactly, not from the source. All machine keys/enums remain unchanged. An annotation never repairs missing question content. Before returning, repeat the source-hidden subject check, final-ask persona check, whole-source conflict check, and complete-span coverage check. Repair or discard any failure; never emit it merely because a decider admitted this persona.

C01 — SOURCE INPUT
publication_number: EP-4634120-A1
[en]
Title: Positive electrode active material and method for manufacturing a positive electrode active material

Abstract: The present invention relates a positive electrode active material for solid-state batteries, comprising lithium, oxygen, nickel, and at least one metal selected from the group consisting of manganese and cobalt, wherein the positive electrode active material has an enriched amount of Si in the surface layer, and wherein the positive electrode active material comprises single-crystalline particles. The present inventors have surprisingly found that the positive electrode active material of the invention improves the storage stability of the positive electrode active material. In particular, a decreased uptake of water and carbon (or carbon dioxide) is observed by applying a surface layer of Si on the positive electrode active material. Moreover, the positive electrode active material improves the electrochemical stability of the battery.

Valid output:
[
  {
    "question": "What storage-related effect is reported for silicon surface enrichment of single-crystalline cathode particles in solid-state batteries?",
    "answer": "In particular, a decreased uptake of water and carbon (or carbon dioxide) is observed by applying a surface layer of Si on the positive electrode active material.",
    "anchor": "silicon surface enrichment of single-crystalline cathode particles",
    "evidence": "In particular, a decreased uptake of water and carbon (or carbon dioxide) is observed by applying a surface layer of Si on the positive electrode active material.",
    "evidence_language": "en",
    "answer_is_translation": false,
    "relationship_type": "modification_effect"
  }
]

Why this fits: A stated intervention is connected to a reported storage-related effect. The question asks for that effect, not a molecular mechanism or an independently verified guarantee.

Near miss to reject or repair:
By what molecular mechanism does silicon completely prevent moisture uptake in cathode particles?
Reason: Neither the molecular mechanism nor complete prevention is stated. A report of decreased uptake cannot supply either premise.
