articles_involved contains the target and exactly the units needed for the answer or indispensable legal clarification. Same-act articles: bare numbers. Other-act articles and ALL annexes: exact Cite as keys. Do not declare unused citations or duplicate language versions.
Preserve obligation, permission, prohibition, discretion, actor, conditions and exceptions. A necessary condition is not sufficient; no converse, unstated sanction, invented motive or national implementation. Duties and safeguards do not prove compliance or efficacy.
All modes exclude pure transposition, general commencement/application, addressee/binding-entirety, repeal, generic committee/collection formalities and generic review/audit/delegation/penalty/data-protection boilerplate. A date, comparison, claim wrapper or source-finding clue cannot rescue excluded formal content. A mixed article's substantive content can qualify. Concrete institutional safeguards or accountability mechanisms can qualify for conceptual and the new modes; a heading alone never excludes them. Lookup and fact_pattern additionally retain the existing categorical exclusions of institutional review/report/evaluation, financial-interests/audit access and delegation clauses. These mode-specific restrictions do not exclude a substantive institutional target from every mode.

CLAIM_VERIFICATION: one plausible assertion is explicitly UNCERTAIN; the evidence determines supported or contradicted. The verdict, not necessarily a proposed number, is unknown. Prefer a short direct check of a substantive actor, scope, condition, status or timing distinction. A supported assertion is not automatically trivial. Do not paste a paragraph into “Can the claim that ... be verified?”, mechanically negate facts, force false claims or invent a real briefing/claimant.
SUPPORTED: all material parts of the scoped assertion are established. CONTRADICTED: explicit incompatible evidence at the same scope, time and attribution; an explicit in-scope counterexample can refute a universal claim. Silence, partial support, a missing reference or mere permission is not contradiction. A dated “no decision” does not mean “never”. Check what an attributed source records, not the independent truth of an allegation. Indeterminate claims are skipped, not labelled false.
answer is the deciding contiguous excerpt, without an added verdict/correction. claim_status is supported or contradicted. claim is the exact question substring expressing the assertion; surrounding question can supply scope. anchor is a substantive exact question substring. No opposite-label pair of the same fact. An uncertain false proposition is not an invented historical fact asserted as true.

EVIDENCE AND LANGUAGE
Except for source_finding's identity answer, answer is ONE contiguous span from ONE allowed source unit, read with the question, governing clause and permitted clarification. Adjacent sentences and the shortest necessary intervening text are allowed. Never stitch, reorder, insert ellipses, calculate or write a synthesis. A fragment, lowercase beginning, source list numbering or terminal punctuation is not a defect. Do not trim “solely”, “only if”, “at least”, a relevant exception or a scope condition when that changes the rule. A reference to a list or method does not answer a request for its contents: find the actual supplied substance, narrow the ask or skip.
Use the supplied English version for wording. If absent, faithfully translate one allowed span; do not summarize or invent a corpus version. For comparison/claim_verification set answer_is_translation, and for source_finding set evidence_is_translation: false when copying an available output-language version; true only when translating because it is absent. Other modes keep their existing fields; the same translation rule applies. Preserve legal distinctions, names, values, units, ranges, dates and threshold inclusivity. Different-language versions are the same evidence, not independent sources. Do not silently reconcile material discrepancies.

STANDALONE QUESTIONS
The question alone must identify its activity, programme, mission, issue or process. Replace generic “a programme”, “the Commission”, “the resolution” with the shortest sufficient substantive identification. Preserve necessary technical terms and names; reformulate ordinary scaffolding without a synonym quota or fixed word limit. Do not create fingerprints from addresses, full titles, entire lists or unrelated adjacent facts. A programme name is not automatically a forbidden instrument identifier. An annotation cannot repair a missing question anchor.
Use time explicitly linked to the relevant source, event, request or application period. A cited letter's date is not the target's adoption date. A directory year is not evidence of adoption; do not convert a session number into a calendar year. Publication, adoption, entry into force and implementation differ. A supplied session/event can identify the instance without inventing a year. If an indispensable temporal distinction is unavailable, skip that need. Never reveal the requested deadline just to add time. Do not claim current law, corpus-wide uniqueness or unseen duplicate documents.

[EN] Article 41 — Legal effect of electronic time stamps
  Act: Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 July 2014 on electronic identification and trust services for electronic transactions in the internal market and repealing Directive 1999/93/EC
  Location: CHAPTER III TRUST SERVICES › SECTION 6 Electronic time stamps
Legal effect of electronic time stamps

1. An electronic time stamp shall not be denied legal effect and admissibility as evidence in legal proceedings solely on the grounds that it is in an electronic form or that it does not meet the requirements of the qualified electronic time stamp.

2. A qualified electronic time stamp shall enjoy the presumption of the accuracy of the date and the time it indicates and the integrity of the data to which the date and time are bound.

3. A qualified electronic time stamp issued in one Member State shall be recognised as a qualified electronic time stamp in all Member States.
